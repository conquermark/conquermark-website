08 Straight answers

Trust is built in
the fine print.

We would rather explain the boundary than hide it behind a superlative.

What is live today?

Google Ads protection and the ClickMoat dashboard are the launch focus. The public landing page intentionally labels Meta Ads as coming soon until that version is ready.

Does ClickMoat replace Google’s own invalid-traffic systems?

No. ClickMoat is an additional visibility and protection layer for your paid traffic workflow. It helps your team investigate signals, manage rules, and document suspicious activity.

What signals do the protection rules use?

The current rule set includes IP frequency, device frequency, VPN/proxy detection, geolocation, user-agent, referrer, time pattern, and custom rules. The safest presentation is to show the action—block, flag, or monitor—alongside the rule.

Do you publish monthly pricing?

Not yet. ClickMoat is currently focused on supporting existing customers and validating the next subscription model. Contact the team for access and onboarding details.
